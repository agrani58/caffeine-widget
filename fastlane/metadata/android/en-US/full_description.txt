A minimal home-screen widget that keeps your Android screen awake when you need it.

Tap the cup and your screen stops timing out - perfect for reading, recipes, presentations, or maps. Tap again and your original timeout is restored exactly as it was.

Features:
* One tap to toggle screen timeout
* Remembers and restores your previous timeout setting
* Tiny: 44 KB, zero dependencies
* No ads, no trackers, no analytics
* No internet permission - it cannot send any data anywhere
* No background services - zero battery impact
* One permission only: Modify system settings (required to change screen timeout)

Works on Android 8.0+. Add the widget from your launcher's widget picker.

Developed with AI assistance and human testing.
